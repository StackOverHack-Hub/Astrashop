# Shopkeeper pitch

When a student needs a particular record book, drawing supply, or lab item, they may call or come by to check whether you have it. We’re testing a simple way to help them check availability and reserve the right item before they make the trip.

Would you try Astrashop with us for a short pilot at your counter? We’d start with a small list of items you already sell and agree on a pickup window that fits your shop hours. You keep your normal prices and payment process. Students pay at the counter.

## What the trial involves

1. We help list the exact items, prices, and pickup hours. You confirm the number of units on the shelf that are still available to sell.
2. When a student reserves a unit, it appears on the shop screen with a six-digit code. Check the screen during the agreed hours and put the exact item aside.
3. Match the student’s code when they arrive and mark the item collected. If they miss the window, put the unit back and release the hold in Astrashop.

You can tell us at any point if the steps are taking too long or interfering with normal sales. We’ll measure whether students get a reliable answer faster and collect the item on time. We’ll ask you before naming your shop or using a quote in the demo.
