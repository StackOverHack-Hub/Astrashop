## Who it is for

The first users are [COURSE OR COHORT] students at [CAMPUS] looking for specific supplies such as [VALIDATED ITEM]. The pilot serves one campus shop, [PILOT SHOP], whose staff confirm shelf stock and set reserved units aside during the agreed pickup hours.

## How it works

Students search by course or item and see the price, shelf stock, when stock was last confirmed, and the pickup window. They reserve one unit and receive a six-digit code. The shopkeeper sees the hold, sets the physical item aside, checks the code at pickup, and marks the hold collected. If a hold lapses under the shop’s pickup policy, the shopkeeper returns the item to the shelf and releases the hold. Students pay in person.

The value is being able to secure the exact item the course requires before walking to the shop. The pilot tests whether that workflow reduces time and missed pickups compared with calls, messages, or visits.
